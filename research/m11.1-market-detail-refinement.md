## 1. Executive Summary & Design Ethos

Milestone 11.1 executes an uncompromising, subtractive beauty and credibility pass on the Web-2.0 Market Detail experience. Rather than introducing speculative decorative UI, gradients, glows, or animation, this milestone refines the interface through:
- **Subtraction**: Eliminating duplicate static boxes, cold-start `Buy|Sell` toggles, internal development badges, pulsing radar dots, and double-stacked mobile bottom bars.
- **Hierarchy & Transparency**: Pairing share prices (`65¢`) with implied probability (`~65%`), introducing explicit downside loss communication (`-$25.00 loss`), and displaying untruncated resolution sources.
- **Restraint & Truth**: Selecting an honest 0–100% chart domain with a 50% toss-up midpoint baseline, prioritizing institutional credibility over artificial curve drama.

$$\text{LESS UI} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{MORE HIERARCHY} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{MORE CLARITY} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{MORE CREDIBILITY}$$

## 3. Key Design Decisions

### A. The Chart-Domain Rule: `HONEST SCALE > DRAMATIC CURVE`
- **Initial Suggestion**: Auto-scale Y-axis tightly around current prices (e.g. 55% to 70%).
- **Deliberate Rejection**: In binary prediction markets, probability lines represent odds bounded between 0% (impossible) and 100% (certainty). Tight auto-scaling would visually transform a trivial 2% sentiment fluctuation into a terrifying cliff, deceiving retail users into perceiving extreme volatility where none exists.
- **Rule Adopted**: 
  $$\text{Y-Axis Domain} = [0, 100] \quad \text{with Ticks at } [25\%, 50\%, 75\%]$$
  This establishes the 50% line as the permanent, intuitive toss-up baseline, allowing genuine market consensus to be read accurately.

### B. Mobile Bottom Navigation: `1 SURFACE = 1 INTENT`
- **Problem**: Stacking the global tab bar underneath the sticky trade bar cost 112px of viewport height on iPhone displays, leaving barely 200px of visible reading space.
- **Rule Adopted**: When a user enters `/markets/:id`, they enter an active *execution terminal*. Global app exploration is secondary to evaluating the contract. By suppressing the 4-tab bar on market detail pages, the trade bar anchors cleanly to `bottom-0`, recovering 56px of vertical reading space.
- **Escape Route**: The header's `< Markets` back button and the post-trade `[ Explore More Markets ]` button provide instant escape to the catalog without bottom-chrome clutter.

## 4. Double-Entry Accounting Verification

All calculations in `TradeContext.tsx` were re-audited and reconciled across all tests:

- **Initial Sandbox Capital**: `$10,000.00 USDC`
- **Executed Order**: `$25.00` on YES @ `65¢`
- **Updated Balance**: `$9,975.00 USDC` (Exact deduction)
- **Shares Generated**:
  $$\text{Shares} = \frac{\$25.00}{\$0.65} = 38.4615 \quad (\text{Display: } 38.46 \text{ shares})$$
- **Potential Payout**:
  $$\text{Gross Payout} = 38.4615 \times \$1.00 = \$38.46$$
- **Net Profit Calculation**:
  $$\text{Net Profit} = \$38.46 - \$25.00 = +\$13.46 \quad (+53.8\% \text{ ROI})$$
- **Downside Risk Calculation**:
  $$\text{Loss if NO Wins} = -\$25.00 \quad (\$0.00 \text{ Payout})$$
- **Early Exit Proceeds**:
  $$\text{Proceeds} = 38.4615 \times \$0.65 \times 0.98 = \$24.50 \quad (2\% \text{ exit liquidity fee})$$
